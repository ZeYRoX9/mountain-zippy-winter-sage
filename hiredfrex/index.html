About Hiredfrex

Hiring should be fair, fast and free.

Hiredfrex is an international job platform that connects people with roles at every level — entry to leadership. It's free for candidates and employers, by design.

Why we built it

Most job boards bury good people. Generic keyword filters reject strong candidates over a missing buzzword, and employers drown in unsorted applications. We thought the matching layer could be dramatically better — transparent for candidates, instant for employers, and honest about how it works.

So we built something simpler and more honest: a clean place where verified listings meet real applicants. Employers post in minutes and see every applicant with their CV in one dashboard. Candidates apply free — with an uploaded CV or one built in seconds with our AI CV builder — and track everything in theirs.

Frequently asked questions

Questions? Answered.

Everything candidates and employers ask us most.

For job seekers

Is HiredFrex free for candidates?
Yes — completely free, always. Browsing, applying, and the AI CV builder cost nothing. We never charge job seekers for anything.
Do I need an account to apply?
No. You can apply directly from any job page. If you don't have an account yet, we create one automatically so you can track your application — you'll get a quick email to verify it.
How does the free AI CV builder work?
Answer a few questions about your target role, experience and skills, and we draft a clean, recruiter-ready CV in seconds. You can edit it, copy it, or attach it directly when you apply.
Can I track my applications?
Yes — sign in and your dashboard shows every application you've submitted and its status, plus any jobs you've saved for later.
Are jobs on HiredFrex checked before they're posted?
Yes — employer accounts are created instantly, but every job listing goes through a quick review before it's visible to candidates, so you won't see spam or scam listings.

For employers

How do I get employer access?
Create an account instantly — start here, choose "I'm hiring," add your company name, and you're in. Your first posting goes through a quick review before it's visible to candidates.
Does it cost anything to post a job?
While we're onboarding our first employers, posting is free. We'll always give advance notice before introducing any paid plans.
How do I review applicants?
Your employer dashboard lists every applicant per job, with their note and CV available to download directly.
Can I close or reopen a listing?
Yes, anytime from "My Jobs" in your dashboard — closed listings stop accepting applications but stay in your history.

Account & privacy

Is my data safe?
Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text, and accounts are protected by email verification. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and why.
I forgot my password — now what?
Use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page. We'll email a 6-digit code to reset it.
Still have a question?
Email us anytime at support@hiredfrex.com or use the Contact form — we reply within one business day.

Privacy Policy

Updated 1 July 2026

1. Who we are

HiredFrex.com ("HiredFrex", "we", "us") operates an international job platform connecting job seekers and employers. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to job seekers, employers, and visitors to our site.

2. Data we collect

Account data: name, email address, phone number (optional), hashed password, and account role. Job seeker data: your CV file or CV Builder content (work history, education, skills, languages, certifications), cover notes, saved and applied jobs. Employer data: company name, contact email, job listings you post. Usage data: pages viewed, searches, device and browser information, collected to keep the service secure and improve it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

3. How we use it

To create and secure your account, let employers and job seekers connect, generate CV drafts with AI when you use the CV Builder, verify accounts by email, respond to messages you send us, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiling.

4. Who we share it with

When you apply to a role, the employer who posted it can see your application, contact details, and any CV you attach. We use a small number of trusted service providers to run HiredFrex, who process data only on our instructions and only as needed to provide their service: Neon (database hosting), Netlify (application hosting), Anthropic (Claude AI, used only when you use the CV Builder), and an email provider for account verification and notifications. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data where required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

5. International data transfers

HiredFrex serves users worldwide, including the UAE and EU/UK, and our service providers may process data in different countries. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on the provider's own compliance safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) and select providers that maintain recognised security and privacy standards.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. We aim to respond to any request within 30 days. To exercise these rights, email support@hiredfrex.com, or delete your account at any time from account settings. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Retention

We keep account and application data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal, tax, or dispute-resolution needs, then delete or anonymise it. You can request earlier deletion at any time, subject to any legal retention obligations.

8. Security & breach response

We protect data with encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and access controls limiting who can view your information. If a data breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify affected users and, where required, the relevant regulator, without undue delay.

9. Cookies and advertising

We set a single essential session cookie when you sign in, used only to keep you signed in securely. Your dark-mode preference and cookie-notice acknowledgement are stored in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to HiredFrex and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. You can also learn how Google uses cookies at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking the essential session cookie means you will not be able to stay signed in, but the rest of the site will keep working. See our Cookie Policy for further detail.

10. Changes to this policy

We review this policy periodically and update it as our service or the law changes; the date above reflects the last update. This policy is written to be clear and complete, but for regulated activities or disputes we recommend seeking independent legal advice specific to your situation — as with any online service, this shouldn't be treated as legal advice itself.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@hiredfrex.com.
